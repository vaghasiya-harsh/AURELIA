THE GENEVA ORIGINS

Born in Geneva. Refined by Time.

Founded in the historic Saint- Gervais district, Aurelia began as a quiet rebellion against mass production. Our 1904 mandate was simple: create the perfect gear, one at a time. Through two world wars and the quartz revolution, our commitment to

the mechanical heartbeat remained unshaken. Today, the same limestone walls of our atelier house the fourth generation of master watchmakers, preserving techniques that digital algorithms could never replicate.

A Century of Mastery

Each Aurelia movement is hand- finished by a single master watchmaker over four months. We believe that true luxury cannot be rushed; it must be coaxed from raw metal through patience and skill. From the mirror-polished bevels of

the bridges to the hand-painted indices, every micron is considered. Our atelier remains one of the few that still practices traditional Anglage finishing, ensuring that the interior beauty matches the exterior precision.

Aurelia limits annual global manufacture to precisely 350 timepieces across all references to uphold uncompromising micro-mechanical finishing standards. Standard waitlist times range from 3 to 9 months depending on dial enamel choices and bespoke engravings.
Every Aurelia movement is certified with the prestigious Poinçon de Genève. This legally protected hallmark guarantees that the watch was hand-assembled in the Canton of Geneva and adheres to strict standards regarding polished chamfers, polished screwheads, and timing precision.
